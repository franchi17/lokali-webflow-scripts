Booking & hours:
let neighbors book you directly
Show your weekly hours on your storefront, and add your own scheduling link (Calendly, Acuity, Square Appointments, whichever you already use) so customers book a time without a single back‑and‑forth message. Included with Pro and Featured plans.
The big idea
Your scheduler, our storefront
You probably already run your bookings somewhere: a Calendly page, an Acuity calendar, Square Appointments. Lokali doesn’t replace it. Paste your link in Dashboard → Availability and your storefront gets a “Book an appointment” button that opens your scheduler. Every booking lands where it always has: in your own tool, with your reminders, your intake questions, your policies. On the Featured plan, a Calendly or Acuity link goes one step further: the scheduler opens right inside your storefront page, so neighbors pick a time without leaving Lokali (every other link, and every other plan, keeps the button).
Secure links only. The link must start with https://. That’s the address your scheduler’s own “share” button gives you. Anything else won’t save.
Step one
Grab your link from the tool you use
Each scheduler has a share link built in. Copy it, then paste it into the Booking link card on your Availability page and tap Save.
Calendly
Share → Copy link
looks like calendly.com/your‑name; use your main page or one event type
Acuity / Squarespace
Scheduling page link
under Client’s Scheduling Page, the direct link clients use to book
Square Appointments
Online booking site
your square.site booking address from Online Booking → Channels
Use a different tool? Any secure booking link works: Setmore, Booksy, your own website’s booking page. If people can book you at that address, it belongs in the box.
Your schedule
Hours: set them once, they show every week
The Hours section is one weekly schedule: for each day, add the window you’re open (like 9:00 to 5:00). It repeats every week and shows on your storefront as your hours of operation, right under the booking button.
1
Closing for lunch? Add two windows
Add 9:00–12:00 and 1:00–5:00 on the same day. Customers see the split hours, so no one shows up over your break.
2
Set your week once with Copy
Fill in one day, tap Copy, tick the other days it applies to, and Apply. A day with no windows simply shows as Closed.
3
Keep them honest, not aspirational
Hours are a promise to a neighbor who might drive over. If your scheduler and your hours disagree, customers trust the hours. Update both when your week changes.
Books full?
The “Accepting new clients” switch
When you can’t take anyone new, flip Accepting new clients off on the New clients card. Your storefront swaps the booking button for a books-full note, and your hours stay visible for existing clients. On the Featured plan, new customers can add themselves to your new-client waitlist from that note, and when you have room you tap Offer spot to invite the next person: they get an email with 6 hours to claim it. The queue is yours to tidy, too: Remove clears an entry you’ve already dealt with (they can always rejoin later), and Withdraw takes back an offer you’ve sent, putting that person back in line. Both ask you to tap twice, so a stray tap never drops a real customer.
The switch works on every plan: any vendor can say “books full.” It’s only the waiting list behind it that Featured unlocks, so the demand you turn away still becomes your next warm leads.
Checklist

2

3
