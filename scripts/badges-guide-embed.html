The Lokali Community
Badges on Lokali:
what they mean
Little marks of recognition for the people who make the neighborhood work — the customers who show up for local businesses, and the vendors who show up for their customers. Here's every badge, how it's earned, and the rules we hold ourselves to.
Our promise
Badges are earned, never bought
Three rules govern every badge on Lokali, and they're non-negotiable:
Rule one No badge is for sale Recognition comes from real activity. There's no way to pay for any badge on this page.
Rule two Badges carry no perks Customer badges never earn discounts or rewards — so you can trust that every review was written freely, not farmed for points.
Rule three No negative labels, ever Badges celebrate. We never publish "slow to respond" or any downgrading mark — the absence of a badge is as negative as it gets.
For customers
Customer badges
These appear on your account and, for some, next to your name on reviews you write. They recognize the neighbors who explore, review, and spread the word.
Neighborhood Explorer You've looked everywhere Earned by exploring vendors in every category on The Market — from food to photography. The full tour.
First Review Your first review You wrote your first review of a local business. Every storefront's reputation starts with someone like you.
Neighborhood Regular Five reviews strong Earned at five reviews. Regulars are the backbone of trustworthy local reputations.
Neighborhood Scout You found them first Earned by being among the first three people to review a vendor. Scouts give new businesses their first proof — you can earn this one again and again.
Neighborhood Connector Your shares bring people in Earned when links you've shared bring five visitors to local storefronts. Word of mouth, measured honestly — we count real visits, not taps.
Why no rewards? The moment a badge earns you a discount, every review behind it becomes suspect. Status-only badges keep Lokali reviews worth reading.
For vendors
Vendor recognition
Vendor recognition works differently — most of it is private, for your eyes only.
Milestones Private, just for you Your dashboard quietly records five early firsts: first save, first inquiry, first 100 views, first review, and first customer share. They never appear on your public listing — they're a record of your business taking root, visible only to you.
Community Builder You grew the neighborhood Earned by bringing three other local businesses onto Lokali through your referral link. Every referral counts toward this — whether they join free or paid.
Responds quickly Coming to listings soon A badge that appears automatically on vendors who consistently reply to inquiries within about a day. It comes and goes on its own based on your recent replies — and true to rule three, there is no "slow to respond" counterpart. If you don't have the badge, your listing simply shows nothing.
The referral program
Bring your neighbors, earn free months
Every vendor has a personal referral link on their dashboard. Know another local business that belongs on Lokali? Send it to them. Here's exactly how the rewards work:
The reward 1 month free For each neighbor who joins a paid plan through your link — credited at your own plan's rate, applied to your subscription bill.
The cap 12 months / year Credits max out at 12 free months in any rolling 12-month period — enough to make Lokali completely free, not a currency.
Recognition Every join counts Free-plan joins don't earn credit, but they count toward Community Builder. Growing the neighborhood matters either way.
The fine print, plainly: each new vendor can be referred by one person (the first link they used); referring yourself or your own storefront doesn't count; credits are applied to your billing when your referred neighbor's paid subscription becomes active. Questions? Contact us.
