Import your products:
bring your whole catalog over in one sitting
Already selling on Etsy, Shopify, or your own site? Export your listings once, upload the file, and Lokali creates your products for you, photos and buy links included. You finish each one with the local details, then switch it on. Included with the Featured plan.
The big idea
Your shop stays where it is. Lokali is your local storefront.
Nothing moves and nothing gets reconnected. Your Etsy or Shopify shop keeps running exactly as it does today, and checkout stays there too. What the import gives you is a local storefront in front of neighbors who browse by city and category: each imported product carries a Buy on Etsy or Buy online button that sends shoppers to your listing to pay, while Inquire stays on every product for custom and bulk orders. The file is read in your browser and never uploaded to us; only the products you tick are created.
Curate, don’t dump. A local storefront works best with your 20 to 40 strongest items, not 800 of them. Tick the ones a neighbor would want to find, and let the buy link carry them to the full catalog.
Step one
Export your listings from the shop you already run
Every platform can hand you a spreadsheet of your listings. Save it somewhere you can find it; it is the file you will upload in step two.
Etsy
Download your listings
Shop Manager → Settings → Options → Download Data → Currently for sale listings (CSV)
Shopify
Products → Export
choose all products and “Plain CSV file”; product links come over automatically
Any spreadsheet
Save as CSV
a Title column is all it needs; Description, Price, Quantity and image links are used when present
Step two
Upload it and tick what you want
Go to Dashboard → Products and click Import from spreadsheet, next to Add product. Pick your file and a preview appears before anything is created.
1
Add your shop onceEtsy: type your shop name (the part after etsy.com/shop/). Shopify: type your store address. That is what turns every imported product into a working buy link. Lokali remembers it for next time.
2
Check the ticksEvery new row is ticked. Rows whose title already exists in your products are marked Already listed and left unticked, so you can re-import a fresh export later and only the new items come in.
3
Import as draftsClick the import button and watch the count climb. Photos are copied one product at a time, so a big file takes a minute or two. Keep the page open until it finishes.
What comes over
Six things arrive, three stay behind
The import fills in what your file can tell us. The rest is yours to add, because it is local knowledge no export contains.
Comes overTitle
Up to 140 characters, exactly as exported. Worth trimming later into plain shopper language.
Comes overDescription
Full text. Shopify formatting is flattened to plain paragraphs.
Comes overPrice and quantity
A blank or zero price becomes “Price on request”. Quantity fills your stock count.
Comes overPhotos
Up to five per product, the first one as the cover. If a photo cannot be copied, the product still imports.
Comes overBuy link
Shopify gets the exact product page. Etsy gets a link that opens your shop searched for that title; swap in the exact listing link whenever you like.
Comes overShipping
Every import is marked as shipping, since that is how an online shop sells. Add pickup or delivery yourself.
Stays behindTags and materials
Lokali uses a Specialty instead. Pick one per product; it is how neighbors find you.
Stays behindVariations and SKUs
Sizes, colors and stock codes live on your shop. Mention options in the description if they matter.
Stays behindPickup, delivery, lead time
Local details only you know. Set them on each product before you switch it on.
Step three
Finish each draft, then switch it on
Imported products land in your Inactive list, switched off, so nothing half-finished shows on your storefront. Open each one and give it the two-minute treatment.
1
Check the photosReorder them, drop the weak ones, and set the focal point on the cover so it crops well on the card.
2
Pick a SpecialtyOne per product. This is what search and the category filters use, so it matters more than any tag did.
3
Set the local optionsPickup available, local delivery, lead time. This is what makes a neighbor choose you over the same item shipped from anywhere.
4
Say it like a neighbor wouldMarketplace titles are written for search engines. “Hand-lettered birthday card” beats a 140-character keyword string.
5
Switch it onTick Active and save. It goes to the top of your storefront in the order it came from your file. Star up to six as your picks.
One at a time
Adding a single product from a link
No spreadsheet handy? Open Add product, paste the product’s page link into the Buy link field, and click Fill from link. The title, description, price and cover photo fill in from the page for you to review. Works for Shopify, Squarespace, Faire, Amazon and your own website. Etsy links get the Buy on Etsy button but do not fill in, because Etsy does not allow it; use the spreadsheet for those.
Good to know. Files up to 8 MB and 500 rows. Re-importing is safe: anything already listed is skipped. Drafts you do not want can be deleted from the Inactive list. Nothing is ever changed on your Etsy or Shopify shop.
