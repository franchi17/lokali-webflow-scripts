Your profile photo:
make a great first impression
No photographer, no ring light, no problem. Here's how to put your best foot forward, in under five minutes.
What works
Three photos that always land well
Your profile photo is the first thing people see. It doesn't have to be perfect. It just has to feel real and clear.
Great
A clear headshot
Face visible, good light, simple background. Friendly goes a long way.
Great
A photo of your work
Cakes, flowers, a finished room. Let what you do speak for you.
Great
Your logo
Clean logo on a white or light background. Simple and instantly professional.
Skip this
Group photos
People won't know which one is you, and that's the whole point.
Skip this
Dark or blurry
Hard to see means hard to trust. A window and 30 seconds is all it takes.
Skip this
Busy backgrounds
A cluttered background competes with you. You should be the main event.
Quick tips
Four things that make a real difference
None of these require anything you don't already have.
1
Use natural light
Sit near a window with the light facing you, not behind you. Your phone camera will handle the rest. No ring light needed.
2
Find a plain background
A white wall, a tidy kitchen counter, a clean outdoor spot. Simple backgrounds keep the focus where it belongs: on you.
3
Think square, think centered
Your photo displays in a circle on Lokali. Center your face or logo in the frame so nothing important gets cropped out.
4
Real beats polished
An attorney at their desk. A plumber on the job. A woodworker at the bench. Authentic photos build more trust than studio shots.
