Quick tips
Four habits for better service shots
None of these require anything you don't already have.
1
Use natural light
Shoot near a window or outdoors in daylight. Soft, even light makes your work look its best, far better than a phone flash.
2
Show real results
Photograph your actual jobs, not stock images. A snapshot of work you really did is the most convincing thing on the whole listing.
3
Capture the action
Get a shot mid-service when you can. Hands at work, tools in use, a client smiling. It brings the service to life.
4
Center for the square crop
Your cover photo can show as a square in some places. Keep the main subject centered so nothing important sits right at the edge.
