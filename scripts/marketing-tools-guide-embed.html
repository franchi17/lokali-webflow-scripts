Marketing Tools:
the promo button & showcase of the week
Two ways to keep your storefront fresh without touching it every day. You write a handful of entries once, and Lokali shows a different one each week. The promo button comes with Pro and Featured; the Showcase is a Featured perk.
The big idea
How the weekly rotation works
1
One at a time, in your order
Your entries show one at a time, top of your list first. Reorder with the arrows, and the dates on each card update so you can plan weeks ahead.
2
Changes every Monday, automatically
Nothing to schedule and nothing to remember. Every entry in your dashboard shows the week it goes live, and the current one is marked Live this week.
3
Pin one during a promotion
A pinned entry stays up until you switch back to rotating, perfect for a launch week or a holiday special.
4
Hide instead of delete
Hiding skips an entry without losing it, so seasonal ones can come back next year exactly as you wrote them.
Tool one · Pro & Featured
The promo button
A button at the top of your contact card, the first thing a visitor can tap. Give it a question or an invitation. If you add a link, the button opens it; with no link, it opens your message form so the visitor can reply right there.
Button text
Up to 120 characters
aim for under 40 so it reads as a button, not a sentence
Link (optional)
https:// only
anything else is rejected; leave empty to open your Lokali message form
How many
Up to 20
3–5 active ones is the sweet spot
Clicks
Tracked in Leads
every tap shows in your Leads page, labeled Promo
Ideas to borrow:
Start a conversation
  • “Looking for something specific?”
  • “What can I help you find?”
  • “First time here? Say hi”
Share what’s open now
  • “Two spots left this month”
  • “Fall booking calendar is open”
  • “Holiday orders close Dec 15”
Point somewhere
  • “See this week’s menu”
  • “New work, take a look”
  • “Book your free consult”
Ask & learn
  • “Tell me what you’d love to see”
  • “Ask about custom orders”
  • “Want a quote? It takes 2 minutes”
Questions out-perform statements here. A question invites a reply, and the reply lands in your Leads inbox.
Tool two · Featured
Showcase of the week
A section at the very top of your storefront, above your services, for the one thing you want everyone to see this week: a new item, an event, a favorite piece of work. Photo on top, your headline and a couple of sentences underneath, plus an optional button that links wherever you choose.
Photo
Landscape, under 1 MB
about twice as wide as tall, at least 1200 px wide; JPG or PNG, and we compress automatically, so most photos are fine as-is
Headline & text
120 + 600 characters
“This week: …” is a reliable headline shape; two or three sentences of text
Link & button label
https:// + 40 characters
the link renders as a button on the card. Name it “Pre-order now”, “RSVP”… or leave empty for “Take a look”
How many & clicks
Up to 10
link taps show in Leads, labeled Showcase
Ideas by what you do:
Makers & food
  • This week’s menu or flavor
  • A just-finished custom piece
  • Where to find you Saturday
Service pros
  • A before & after from this week
  • A seasonal package
  • One open slot this week
Beauty & wellness
  • A look you did this week
  • New service on the menu
  • A returning-client special
Events & classes
  • Next date, few seats left
  • Photos from the last one
  • Early-bird ends Friday
Professionals
  • A deadline worth knowing about
  • A question clients ask every week
  • One opening for new clients
The photo does most of the work. If you only change one thing each week, change the photo.
Day to day
Good habits
1
Write 3–5 entries in one sitting
Then let the rotation do the work. Your storefront stays fresh for a month without another visit.
2
Pin during promotions, unpin after
Launch week, holiday special, market weekend: pin it so it stays front and center, then switch back to rotating.
3
Check Leads once a week
The Promo and Showcase labels tell you which entries actually get taps. Keep the winners, rewrite the rest.
