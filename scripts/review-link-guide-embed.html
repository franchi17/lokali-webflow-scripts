Your review link:
ask the customers you meet in person
Most of your customers never tap a button on Lokali. They meet you at a market, at a pickup, or at their front door. Your review link lets those customers leave a review too, in about a minute.
The big idea
One or two reviews change how a stranger reads your storefront
Normally only people who contacted you through Lokali can review you. That keeps reviews honest, and it also means a happy customer you met face to face had no way in. Your review link is that way in. It is personal to your storefront, it never changes, and a review that arrives through it is clearly labeled so shoppers know how it got there.
Where to find it. Open your dashboard, go to Marketing, and look for the card called Ask for a review. The Marketing page is part of the Pro and Featured plans.
How to use it
Three ways to hand it over
The card gives you the link, a QR code, and ready-made wording. Pick whichever fits the moment.
1
Copy the link into a text or emailTap Copy link, or copy the ready-made text message or email. Both already include your link and a short, friendly ask.
2
Print the QR codeDownload it as a PNG or an SVG and put it on your booth sign, a receipt, or a thank-you card. A customer scans it with their phone camera and lands on your reviews.
3
Slip a note in the bagThe packaging note wording is written to be printed small and tucked in with an order.
Ask at the right moment. The best time is right after a pickup or a handoff, while the good feeling is fresh. A week later, most people mean to and never do.
The customer's side
What happens when they open it
Your link opens your storefront on the Reviews section, with a card at the top that says you invited them to leave a review.
1
They tap Write a reviewIf they do not have a Lokali account yet, a short sign-up opens right there. They never leave your page.
2
They choose Would recommend or Would notLokali reviews are a recommendation, not a star rating. A comment is optional.
3
It posts right awayThe review appears on your storefront with their first name and last initial, and you get an email.
Honest labels
What each review label means
Every review on Lokali says how the reviewer reached you. Shoppers trust reviews more when nothing is hidden.
Verified contact
They sent you a message through the Lokali form before reviewing.
Contacted through Lokali
They tapped Call, Text, WhatsApp or Email on your storefront before reviewing.
Invited by the vendor
They came through your review link. If that same person also contacted you through Lokali, they get the contact label instead.
The ground rules
What keeps reviews worth reading
One review per person
Each account can review your storefront once. They can edit it later from their own account.
No reviewing yourself
Your own account cannot review your storefront, even through your link.
You cannot edit or hide a review
If one is fake or abusive, report it from your storefront and a person at Lokali looks at it.
You can reply
Replying to reviews is part of the Pro and Featured plans. A short thank-you goes a long way.
