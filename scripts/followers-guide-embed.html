Vendor Resources
Followers:
keep the customers you have already won
A happy customer walks away from your booth and cannot find you again. Followers fixes that. When a shopper saves your storefront they follow you, and a thirty-second post tells them where you will be and what is new.
The big idea
Saving your storefront is following you
There is nothing new for a shopper to learn. The Save button on your storefront is the follow button. Your QR code and your storefront link both lead there, so every customer you meet can follow you in one tap. Followers is free on every plan.
Where to find it. Open your dashboard and choose Followers, under Grow. You will see how many followers you have, how many are new this week, and how many get the weekly email.
Posting
Four kinds of update, thirty seconds each
Pick what your update is about, fill in a line or two, and press Post. You can post up to ten times a day, and each update can be up to 280 characters.
Where I'll be
The place, the date and the hours. It opens on next Saturday, nine to one, so most weeks you only type the market name. Tick the box if you are there every week and you never have to post it again.
New item
Something back in season, a new flavor, a new service.
Orders open
Holiday pre-orders, a custom order window, a booking slot that just opened.
Sold out
Saves your customers a wasted trip, and tells them when it is back.
Changed your plans? Every post has a Remove link under Your recent posts. It disappears from your storefront straight away.
Where it shows up
One post, three places
1
On your storefront, right awayA section called Where to find you appears near the top, with your next stops for the coming two weeks and your latest update from the last thirty days. When you have nothing current, the section simply is not there.
2
On the This Week pageEvery Where I'll be post for the next nine days is listed at golokali.com/this-week, grouped by day and by place, so shoppers can see who will be at each market.
3
In one weekly email to your followersOn Thursdays, in time for the weekend, Lokali sends each shopper a single email covering all the vendors they saved. One email a week at most, however many vendors they follow.
Privacy
You see a number, never a name
Your Followers page shows counts only. You cannot see or export who follows you, and Lokali never shares a shopper's email with a vendor. Shoppers can switch the weekly email off with one click and still keep you saved. That promise is what makes people comfortable tapping Save in the first place.
Getting followers
Three habits that build the list
1
Put your QR code where people payIt opens your storefront, and the Save button is right there. Say it out loud: save me and you will know where I am next weekend.
2
Post your week before ThursdayThat is when the weekly email goes out, so a Wednesday post reaches people while they are planning their weekend.
3
Use the every week boxIf you are at the same market each Saturday, tick it once. Your storefront and the This Week page stay current without you lifting a finger.
